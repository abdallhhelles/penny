# Penny 1.9.1 Beta

- **Insights, rebuilt.** A monthly recap at the top, findings grouped into *Needs attention*, *Good news* and *Worth knowing*, and a button on each one that does the next step (see the transactions, adjust a budget, add a bill).
- **Smarter findings:** bills due before payday, goals running late (with the monthly amount that fixes it), categories rising three months in a row, income changes, unusual or double charges and price increases.
- **"What changed?"** shows exactly which categories and merchants moved your spending.
- **Snooze or hide** any insight. Switch between this month, 3 months and this year.
- **One health score everywhere**, the same as in the Financial Health Report.
- Top merchants, category trends, clickable months on the income chart, and a what-if tool that shows how much sooner you'd reach a goal.
- **Smoother everywhere:** toggles and changes update in place without flashing, a sliding marker follows the page you're on, each tab remembers where you scrolled, and sheets glide closed.
- Insights is fully translated (English, Spanish, Arabic).
- Small fixes: a "New goal" card, spacing on Transactions, one "New" button instead of two.
