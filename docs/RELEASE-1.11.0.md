# Penny Beta 1 (1.11.0)

The first public beta of Penny: private, offline personal finance for Windows, now in five languages. Everything is free during the beta. Feedback is welcome on Discord: https://discord.gg/r5kNGC6p29

- **Penny speaks five languages.** Every screen, message, notification, report, tip and help page is now available in English, Chinese (Simplified, 简体中文), Hindi (हिन्दी), Spanish (Español) and Arabic (العربية). Pick one in Settings › Language & money, or let Penny follow Windows.
- **Type in your own language.** Smart entry ("昨天在星巴克咖啡 4.5", "कल स्टारबक्स पर कॉफ़ी 4.5"), quick reminders and Ask Penny questions are understood in all five languages, including local digits and decimal commas.
- **Your categories follow your language.** Penny's built-in categories rename themselves when you switch language; any category you renamed yourself stays as you wrote it.
- **Dates and numbers follow your language** by default, and language and country names show in your language.
- **Translated license and privacy notice**, with a button to show the English text, which remains the binding version.
- **The installer speaks your language too** (English, Chinese, Hindi, Spanish or Arabic, chosen from Windows).
- Fixes: chart labels are never cut off in languages with longer number formats; the Financial Health Report's sources are translated; Chinese and Hindi use Windows' own fonts.
