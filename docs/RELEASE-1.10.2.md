# Penny 1.10.2 Beta

- **A clearer Financial Health Report.** It opens with a one-line verdict, your score by area and four key figures, then a numbered action plan with buttons that take you to the right page. A scorecard lists every measure against its benchmark with a clear status (On track, Watch, Act now). New sections show cash flow by month, where your money goes against your budgets, a balance sheet, and when each debt will be paid off. The glossary and long source list are folded away, and the PDF prints cleanly.
- **Penny Pro is marked "coming soon".** Everything stays free during the beta.
- **Safer installer:** Setup no longer modifies Penny.exe. This rules out a step suspected of making existing encrypted data unreadable after installing 1.10.1 (your recovery key unlocks it).
- Fixes: the year on the About page no longer shows as "2,026"; the withdrawal rate shows as 3.9% instead of rounding to 4%; no sideways scrolling on wide pages.
