# Penny brand (v1.0, implemented in app 1.10)

Source of truth: the "Penny Brand Guidelines" deck. This file is the short version for developers.

**Mark:** a copper coin with a 72° evergreen slice lifted 6 units outward, on a 100-unit grid. Flat (two paths) up to 32 px, detailed (gradients, rim, soft shadow) from 48 px. No tile, never rotated, stretched, outlined or recoloured off-palette. Clear space = ¼ of the mark.
Generated by `python3 brand/build_brand.py` → `renderer/logo-mark.js` (`LOGO_MARK(size)`), `assets/icon.ico` (16–256), `assets/icon.png`, `assets/tray-*.png`, installer bitmaps and `brand/*.svg`.

**Wordmark:** "penny", always lowercase, Sora SemiBold, letter-spacing −0.045em. Lockups: horizontal (mark = 1.15 × type, gap ¼ mark) and stacked (mark = 1.75 × type, gap ⅛ mark), outlined in `brand/penny-*-on-*.svg`.

**Colour:** Copper 300/500/700 `#EE9A5C #C9632F #9E4520` · Evergreen 300/500/700 `#2BB89A #12846F #0F7563` · Ink `#14211F` · Night `#10201D` · Slate `#5B6664` · Mist `#A9B8B4` · Sand `#E8DFD3` · Cream `#FBF6EF`. Copper = brand and primary actions; Evergreen = money in, savings, positive.

**Themes (only two):** Light = Cream background, white surfaces, Ink text, copper actions (`#B5532A` for 4.5:1 with white text). Dark = Night background, `#1B302C` surfaces, Cream text, Copper 300 actions with Night text. Tokens live at the end of `renderer/style.css`.

**Type:** Sora (bundled `renderer/sora-latin*.woff2`, SIL OFL, `assets/Sora-OFL.txt`) for titles and amounts with tabular figures; Segoe UI Variable for everything else.

**Electron:** window `backgroundColor` = Night or Cream from the saved theme (no white flash); window icon = `assets/icon.ico`; tray = flat mark in the 500s on light taskbars, the 300s on dark ones.
