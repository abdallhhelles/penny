# Penny 1.10.0 Beta

- **A new look.** Penny has a new identity: a copper penny with one evergreen slice lifted out — your money, with the share you've set aside. New app icon, tray icon and installer art.
- **Two themes, done properly:** Light (cream and ink) and Dark (night and cream). Pick one in Settings › Appearance; switching cross-fades smoothly.
- **Brand type:** titles and amounts use Sora (bundled, works offline); everything else uses the Windows system font so it feels native.
- Copper for actions, evergreen for money in and good news, everywhere — buttons, charts, rings and the sidebar.
- **New profile pictures** in the brand colours: sixteen drawings (fox, owl, sprout, coffee and more) on copper, evergreen or sand, and they follow Light and Dark. Your old pick carries over to the closest match.
- Profile photos are sharper (256 px), transparent PNGs no longer turn black, you can pick the same photo again after removing it, and unreadable files get a clear message.
- **Receipts and files on transactions.** Attach photos, PDFs or documents: use the button in a transaction, drop files on it, paste a screenshot, or right-click › Attach. Files are encrypted like the rest of your data and never leave your PC. Photos open in a built-in viewer.
- **Right-click anywhere** for a menu that fits what you clicked: edit, duplicate, attach or delete a transaction; show an account's or category's transactions; record a bill payment; mark a reminder done; cut, copy and paste in text fields. Works with the keyboard too (Menu key or Shift+F10).
- **Easier to get around:** the sidebar is grouped (Everyday, Plan ahead, Understand) with counts for due reminders, due bills and insights that need you; long pages get an "On this page" bar that follows your scroll; a back-to-top button; arrow keys move through lists on every tab, and / jumps to search.
- **Transactions:** the filter bar stays put while you scroll, day headings stick, and every active filter shows as a chip you can remove with one click.
- **Join the Penny community** on Discord, from the sidebar (you can hide it), Settings › Updates & help, or Ctrl K.
- The window opens in your theme's colour, so there's no white flash on launch.
- Simpler Settings: the accent colours, glass style, backgrounds and blur options are gone.
