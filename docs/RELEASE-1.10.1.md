# Penny 1.10.1 Beta

- **Receipts and files on transactions.** Attach photos, PDFs or documents: use the button in a transaction, drop files on it, paste a screenshot, or right-click › Attach. Files are encrypted like the rest of your data and never leave your PC. Photos open in a built-in viewer.
- **Right-click anywhere** for a menu that fits what you clicked: edit, duplicate, attach or delete a transaction; show an account's or category's transactions; record a bill payment; mark a reminder done; cut, copy and paste in text fields. Works with the keyboard too (Menu key or Shift+F10).
- **Easier to get around:** the sidebar is grouped (Everyday, Plan ahead, Understand) with counts for due reminders, due bills and insights that need you; long pages get an "On this page" bar that follows your scroll; a back-to-top button; arrow keys move through lists on every tab, and / jumps to search.
- **Transactions:** the filter bar stays put while you scroll, day headings stick, and every active filter shows as a chip you can remove with one click.
- **New profile pictures** in the brand colours: sixteen drawings (fox, owl, sprout, coffee and more) on copper, evergreen or sand that follow Light and Dark. Your old pick carries over to the closest match.
- Profile photos are sharper, transparent PNGs no longer turn black, you can pick the same photo again after removing it, and unreadable files get a clear message.
- **The new icon everywhere in Windows**, even when you update from inside the app: Start menu, desktop and pinned taskbar shortcuts, the jump list, the taskbar button and Settings › Apps switch to the new logo on first start.
- **A clearer Financial Health Report.** It now opens with a one-line verdict, your score by area and four key figures, then a numbered action plan with buttons that take you to the right page. A scorecard table lists every measure against its benchmark with a clear status. New sections show cash flow by month, where your money goes against your budgets, a balance sheet, and when each debt will be paid off. The glossary and long source list are folded away, and the PDF prints cleanly.
- **Penny Pro is marked "coming soon".** Everything stays free during the beta.
- **Join the Penny community** on Discord: https://discord.gg/r5kNGC6p29 (also in the sidebar, Settings › Updates & help and Ctrl K).
- Fixes: the year on the About page no longer shows as "2,026"; the withdrawal rate shows as 3.9% instead of rounding to 4%.
